# CCA-F Official Webpage

Everything in the previous two lectures — exam format, registration, proctoring, timing — is written from general knowledge of how technical certification programs run, not from a live copy of Anthropic's exam handbook. This repo is unofficial, self-authored study material, and it will inevitably lag behind the authoritative source on exact, current details.

Before you register, schedule, or pay for the CCA-F, go directly to Anthropic's own sources:

- **[Claude Partner Network & CCA-F announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network)** — Anthropic's official announcement of the certification, launched alongside the $100 million Claude Partner Network investment. This is the authoritative starting point for understanding what the credential is, who it's for, and how it fits into Anthropic's broader certification stack.
- **[Claude Docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/intro)** — the official Claude platform documentation. If a lecture in this course describes a Claude API, Claude Code, or MCP behavior, this is where to verify it's still accurate, since the platform evolves faster than any third-party study guide can track.
- **[Claude Code Docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/overview)** — official documentation specifically for Claude Code, referenced throughout the Tool and Workflow Layer modules.

As of this course's most recent update, CCA-F access runs primarily through Anthropic's Partner Network program, so exact registration steps, pricing, and prerequisites are best confirmed there rather than assumed from this course. That's the single source of truth for the current exam blueprint, live registration details, proctoring requirements, and retake or cancellation policy — all of which can change over time in ways a static study guide can't track. Bookmark these links and check them again close to your exam date.

Use this course to build the architectural judgment the exam tests; use Anthropic's own pages to confirm the mechanics of actually registering for and sitting it. If anything in this repo — a domain weighting, a piece of exam-day logistics, a claim about what a lecture in the official course covers — ever conflicts with what you find on Anthropic's own pages, **treat Anthropic's page as correct and this repo as the thing that needs updating, not the other way around**.

{: .important }
> This repo is unofficial, self-authored study material, not a live copy of Anthropic's exam handbook. Before you register, schedule, or pay for the CCA-F, confirm registration steps, pricing, and the current exam blueprint on Anthropic's own CCA-F page — never take a third-party course's word over the certification body's own materials.
